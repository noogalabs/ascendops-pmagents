# PM-assist edition validation

The property-manager assistant edition is mapping-driven and leaves the sealed
maintenance core (`0540ea08aa8d47ecb1aebbb7f51db85c5a67ab252172804e9ba24e56c2403551`)
byte-identical.

## Five-law resweep

| Law | PM-assist evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Execution assists; judgment stays human | `SOUL.md`, `GUARDRAILS.md`, and the questionnaire keep the PM/broker gates explicit | PASS |
| No configuration answer grants authority | C1/C2 remain recorded intent; `never_graduate` names housing, money, legal, and relationship classes | PASS |

## Skill and SOUL parity

The owner, legal, money, housing, and relationship boundaries in `SOUL.md` are
also present on their executable skill surfaces: `draft-release-gate`,
`fair-housing-guard`, `broker-escalation`, `approval-queue`, and
`escalation-triage`. No skill claims a broader authority than SOUL.

## Cross-seat dispositions

Maintenance-owned deposit deadline, entry notice, SLA, and platform pointers use
the engine's absent-owner hold state. The configured seat records the pending owner
and its local holding answer without activating or fabricating the peer value.
FACT_MATCH, POLICY_DIVERGE, and ORDERING checks are registered independently and
remain inert with `peer_absent` until the maintenance seat is supplied.

### Per-seam engine disposition

The table register is richer than the edition's executable cross-seat mapping.
Every table seam is therefore dispositioned explicitly; absence from
`engine/mappings/pm-assist.json` is never silent.
