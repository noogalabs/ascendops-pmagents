# Leasing Message Template Library

Source: owner-reviewed operating-library baseline, 2026-08-20. These are drafts with
fill-in markers. A human approves every applicant, resident, owner, or prospect message
before it is sent.

## Message controls

- Replace every bracketed field from verified property and contact data.
- Preserve required fair-housing, adverse-action, and local legal language.
- Never infer a deadline, approval, denial, rent, or notice period.
- Run applicant- and resident-facing drafts through the fair-housing guard.
- Store the approver and approval timestamp with the final draft.

## Prospect showing confirmation

Subject: Your showing is confirmed — [Property Address]

Hi [First Name], your showing at [Property Address] is confirmed for [Date] at
[Time]. To reschedule, reply here or use [Reschedule Link]. — [Company Name]

Tone: clear, friendly, and easy to reschedule.

## Incomplete application reminder

Subject: Action needed for your application — [Property Address]

Hi [First Name], your application for [Property Address] still needs [Missing Items].
You can complete it at [Application Link]. If you need help, contact [Phone Number].
— [Company Name]

Tone: helpful and factual, never punitive.

## Renewal Offer — approval required before send

Subject: Your lease renewal options — [Property Address]

Hi [First Name], your lease at [Property Address] ends on [Lease End Date]. The
property manager has prepared these approved options:

- [Term 1]: [Start Date]–[End Date], [Monthly Rent]
- [Term 2]: [Start Date]–[End Date], [Monthly Rent]
- [Month-to-Month Option, if approved]: [Monthly Rent]

Please respond by [Decision Deadline] using [Renewal Response Link] or contact
[Phone Number] with questions. — [Company Name]

Tone: appreciative, neutral, and explicit about options and deadline.

## Renewal reminder

Subject: Reminder: renewal response requested — [Property Address]

Hi [First Name], this is a reminder that the response deadline for the approved
renewal options at [Property Address] is [Decision Deadline]. Please respond at
[Renewal Response Link], whether you plan to stay or leave. — [Company Name]

Tone: neutral and equally accepting of either decision.

## Owner renewal recommendation

Subject: Renewal recommendation — [Property Address]

Hi [Owner Name], the lease at [Property Address] ends on [Lease End Date]. Current
rent is [Current Rent]; the documented market range is [Market Range] as of
[Comparison Date]. We recommend [Path] at [Proposed Rent] for [Term], subject to your
approval. Please approve, counter, or decline by [Owner Deadline]. — [Company Name]

Tone: evidence-based and decision-ready; never present a proposal as already approved.

## Non-renewal preparation notice

Do not send a non-renewal template from this library without property-manager or
counsel approval of the decision, jurisdiction-specific notice period, delivery method,
and exact text. The coordinator prepares the facts and routes the legal notice gate.
