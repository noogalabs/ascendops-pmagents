# Renewal and Rent-Increase Workflow

Source: owner-reviewed operating-library baseline, 2026-08-21. Verify local notice periods, rent-control rules, and every binding decision with the property manager or counsel.

## Operating rule

A renewal signal never becomes a tenant offer until a human approves it.
The coordinator may analyze, recommend, and draft. It never sends a renewal,
non-renewal, rent-increase, or resident-facing message unattended.

## Work backward from lease end

| Target | Preparation step |
|---|---|
| 90–120 days before end | Open the renewal record and verify lease dates. |
| About 90 days | Run the market comparison, review payment and compliance history, and prepare the owner recommendation. |
| After owner approval | Draft the resident offer with rate, terms, and an explicit response deadline. |
| Before the legal notice deadline | Escalate any unresolved renew/non-renew decision to the property manager. |
| After signature | Update the property-management system and preserve the approved record. |

These are planning targets, not legal notice periods. The configured operator supplies
the jurisdiction-specific deadlines and a human confirms them before use.

## Renewal analysis

Prepare one approval packet containing:

- current rent and lease-end date;
- comparable market evidence and its collection date;
- payment, compliance, and material maintenance history;
- proposed path: renew, month-to-month, or non-renew;
- proposed term and rent, clamped to the configured property ceiling;
- any concession expiry, balance, or unresolved owner question; and
- the deadline by which a human decision is required.

Missing or implausible market evidence never justifies a rent change. Recommend holding
flat and explain the missing evidence instead. A non-renewal or legal-notice decision
always stays with the property manager or counsel.

## Owner decision

The owner-facing draft shows the current rate, proposed rate, market support, term
options, resident-history summary, and response deadline. Record the human approver,
decision, and timestamp. A counterproposal returns through the same approval gate.

## Resident offer and negotiation

Only after recorded owner approval, draft the resident offer using the configured
message library. State the proposed rent, term options, response deadline, and reply
route. If the resident counters, prepare the counter for owner approval before any
response. Do not convert silence into consent.

## Decline and unresolved paths

- Resident declines: prepare the approved move-out handoff and vacancy plan.
- Owner declines: route the jurisdiction-specific notice decision to the property manager or counsel.
- No decision by the internal deadline: escalate; never auto-send or auto-renew.
- Missing notice rule: hold the action and surface the configuration gap.

## Tracker controls

Every open lease has a lease-end date, renewal owner, next action, human decision
status, and deadline. Review the tracker at least weekly. No lease is silently dropped
because an automated alert failed or because a response remains ambiguous.
