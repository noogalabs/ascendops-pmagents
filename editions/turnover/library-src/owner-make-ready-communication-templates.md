# Owner Make-Ready Communication Templates

## Owner Make-Ready Communication Templates
Owner Relations — [Company Name]
Purpose: keep owners informed, build trust, and get fast approvals at every key stage of the make-ready process, without them having to chase for updates. Bracketed fields are fill-ins.
### Email 1 - Make-Ready Initiated

---
Send within 24 hours of move-out inspection completion.

Subject: Make-Ready Initiated - [Property Address]
Hi [Owner Name],
Your tenant has vacated [Property Address] and we have completed our move-out inspection. We are kicking off the make-ready process right away to get your unit rent-ready as quickly as possible.
Here is a summary of what we found:
Required repairs (must be completed for rent-readiness):
[Item 1 - estimated cost]
[Item 2 - estimated cost]
[Item 3 - estimated cost]
Cleaning:
Full unit deep clean - [estimated cost]
Carpet shampoo - [estimated cost]
Suggested upgrades (optional; may increase rent potential or leasing speed):
[Item 1 - estimated cost]
[Item 2 - estimated cost]
Compliance items (required by law):
[Item 1 - estimated cost]
Estimated total make-ready cost: [amount]
Estimated completion date: [Date]
Action needed: please reply to approve the required repairs and let us know your decision on any optional upgrades. We will get work orders created and resources scheduled as soon as we hear from you.
Full inspection photos have been uploaded to your owner portal for review. As always, reach out with any questions.
[Your Name]
[Company Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
### Email 2 - Mid-Point Progress Update

---
Send around Day 5 of the make-ready process.

Subject: Make-Ready Update - [Property Address]
Hi [Owner Name],
Quick update on the make-ready progress at [Property Address].
Completed so far:
[Item 1]
[Item 2]
[Item 3]
In progress:
[Item 1] - expected completion [date]
[Item 2] - expected completion [date]
Remaining:
Deep clean scheduled for [date]
Final inspection scheduled for [date]
We are on track to have the unit rent-ready by [estimated completion date].
(If there is a delay) We want to give you a heads up that [item] has caused a slight delay. We now expect completion by [revised date] and are doing everything we can to minimize the impact on your vacancy timeline.
Updated photos will be uploaded to your owner portal following the final inspection.
[Your Name]
[Company Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
### Email 3 - Make-Ready Complete & Marketing Launch

---
Send upon final inspection approval and marketing launch.

Subject: Make-Ready Complete - [Property Address] is Live
Hi [Owner Name],
Great news - [Property Address] has passed its final inspection and is officially rent-ready. We have launched marketing and the unit is now live.
Make-ready summary: all required repairs completed; unit deep cleaned and photo-ready; locks rekeyed; smoke and CO detectors tested and confirmed functioning; HVAC filter replaced; final inspection passed.
Final make-ready cost: [amount] (itemized invoices attached and uploaded to your owner portal)
Marketing is live on:
[Platform 1 - e.g., Zillow]
[Platform 2 - e.g., Apartments.com]
[Platform 3 - e.g., company website]
Listed rent price: [amount]/month
Available date: [Date]
Final photos and the completed make-ready report have been uploaded to your owner portal. We will keep you updated on showing activity and applications as they come in - our goal is to get you a qualified tenant as quickly as possible.
[Your Name]
[Company Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
### Email 4 - Make-Ready Delay Notification

---
Send immediately upon identifying any delay - never let the owner find out on their own.

Subject: Make-Ready Update - Delay at [Property Address]
Hi [Owner Name],
I want to proactively update you on a delay we have encountered at [Property Address].
What happened: [brief, clear explanation - e.g., the flooring vendor we scheduled had an unexpected availability issue and cannot complete the work until [date]].
Impact: our original completion target of [date] will be pushed to approximately [revised date].
What we are doing about it: [e.g., we have already reached out to two additional vendors and are working to secure the earliest available appointment; we will update you as soon as a new date is confirmed].
We apologize for the delay and are doing everything we can to minimize the impact on your vacancy timeline. I will follow up with a confirmed revised completion date by [date].
[Your Name]
[Company Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
### Manager Notes

---
The delay email is the most important one in this set: owners forgive delays they hear about from us first and remember the ones they discover themselves. The Day 5 midpoint update builds trust and prevents surprises on the final invoice.
