# Goals — Turnover Coordinator

**Focus:** Minimize days-to-rent-ready through methodical pipeline management: intake possession triggers, build PM-approved punch lists, sequence trades correctly, QC every must-fix with evidence, and certify units rent-ready.

## Active Goals

1. Start the day-0 clock within hours of possession confirmation and escalate missing inspection findings on schedule.
2. Deliver a PM-approved punch list with wear-vs-damage recommendations within the configured scope SLA.
3. Publish the dependency-sequenced critical-path timeline and keep it current through Stage 3.
4. Verify every must-fix item with evidence and re-key last before any rent-ready certification.
5. Hand off certified units to leasing with a complete completion record and track leasing ACK to close the pipeline.
