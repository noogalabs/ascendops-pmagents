# Make-Ready Checklist

## Make-Ready Checklist
Unit Turnover — [Company Name]
Property: ____________________   Unit: ________   Inspector: ____________________   Date: __________
Purpose: get the unit fully repaired, cleaned, and rent-ready for the next tenant as quickly and efficiently as possible, keeping the owner informed and approved at every key step.
Target timeline: make-ready complete within 7-10 days of vacancy.
### Day 1 - Initiate & Assess
---
Trigger
☐ Move-out final inspection completed and report uploaded to platform
☐ All keys and access devices confirmed returned
☐ Unit confirmed fully vacated
#### Initial Assessment
☐ Review move-out inspection report and photos
☐ Walk the unit and create a full task list categorized into: Cleaning (deep clean, carpet shampoo, odor treatment, sanitization); Repairs (tenant damages, appliance fixes, patching, painting); Upgrades (optional updates for market appeal or rent potential - owner decision); Compliance (anything required by law: smoke detectors, CO detectors, habitability)
☐ Document all findings with photos and upload to owner portal
☐ Rekey locks or update smart lock access - always, no exceptions
#### Owner Communication
☐ Send owner the make-ready assessment: full task list with estimated costs, categorized breakdown (required vs. suggested vs. optional), proposed timeline, and items requiring approval
☐ Obtain owner approval on any costs exceeding the pre-approved spending threshold
☐ Confirm owner's decision on optional upgrades before scheduling
### Days 2-3 - Plan & Assign
---
Work Order Creation
☐ Create individual work orders in the platform for each task
☐ Assign each work order: in-house tech for routine repairs, painting, minor fixes within skill set; vendor for specialized trades (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, flooring); cleaning crew for the full deep clean
☐ Confirm availability of all assigned resources within the make-ready timeline
☐ Confirm materials and supplies are available before scheduling
☐ Get vendor bids for larger scope repairs if required
#### Scheduling
☐ Schedule in the correct sequence: repairs first, painting after repairs, deep clean last, photos after clean
☐ Confirm all scheduled dates with vendors and in-house techs
☐ Log all scheduled dates in the platform
### Days 4-10 - Execute & Monitor
---
Repairs
☐ Walls - patch all holes, repair damage
☐ Paint - touch up or full repaint as needed
☐ Flooring - repair, replace, or refinish as approved
☐ Carpet - shampoo or replace as needed
☐ Doors / locks - repair, adjust, or replace hardware
☐ Windows / screens - repair cracks, replace screens
☐ Blinds / window coverings - replace if damaged
☐ Cabinets / drawers - repair hardware, hinges, doors
☐ Countertops - repair or replace if damaged
☐ Light fixtures / ceiling fans - bulbs, repair, or replace
☐ Outlets / switches - replace covers, repair non-functioning
☐ Smoke detectors - test, replace batteries or unit
☐ Carbon monoxide detectors - test, replace batteries or unit
☐ HVAC filter - replace (every turn, no exceptions)
☐ Plumbing - fix leaks, clear drains, repair fixtures
☐ Appliances - repair or replace as approved by owner
☐ Garbage disposal - test, repair, or replace
☐ Water heater - inspect; note age and condition for owner
☐ Washer / dryer connections - inspect for leaks
☐ Pest control - treat if any evidence of infestation
#### Deep Cleaning (after all repairs and painting complete)
☐ Full unit deep clean - all rooms, surfaces, fixtures
☐ Kitchen - degrease stove, oven, exhaust fan; clean inside refrigerator and dishwasher
☐ Bathrooms - scrub tile, grout, tub, shower, toilet, sink
☐ Carpet shampooing or hard floor refinishing
☐ Odor treatment if needed (pet, smoke, other)
☐ Windows - clean inside and out
☐ Baseboards, doors, and trim wiped down
☐ Cabinets and drawers cleaned inside and out
☐ Garage / patio / exterior areas swept and cleared
☐ Verify cleaning quality with a post-clean walkthrough before approving
#### Progress Monitoring
☐ Check progress on all open work orders at Day 3 - confirm on track
☐ Follow up with any vendor or tech who has not confirmed progress
☐ Escalate any delays to supervisor immediately - never let the timeline slip silently
☐ Update work order statuses in the platform in real time

☐ If any item fails - create a new work order immediately and resolve before proceeding
☐ Take final marketing-quality photos of every room once the unit passes
☐ Upload final photos and inspection report to owner portal and platform
### Day 12 - Owner Sign-Off & Marketing Launch
---
Owner Communication
☐ Send owner make-ready completion notification: summary of work, total costs with invoices, final photos, recommended list price if applicable, proposed marketing launch date
☐ Obtain owner confirmation that the unit is approved for listing
#### Marketing Launch
☐ Approve property as rent-ready in the system
☐ Send final photos and property details to marketing
☐ Confirm listing live on all approved platforms
☐ Notify leasing that the unit is available and ready to show
#### Final Documentation
☐ Mark all make-ready work orders complete and closed
☐ Upload all invoices, photos, and completion confirmations
☐ Log total make-ready cost in the owner's account
☐ File the completed make-ready checklist in the property record

### Manager Notes

---
Sequence matters: repairs, then paint, then clean, then photos, in that order every time - cleaning before repairs finish is wasted money. Replace the HVAC filter every single turn. Never launch marketing with old photos after a unit changes. The 7-10 day target is the vacancy cost control: every vacant day is lost owner revenue. Keep the owner in the loop at every stage - a Day 5 midpoint update builds trust and prevents surprises on the final invoice.
