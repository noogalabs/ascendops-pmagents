# Monthly Turnover Performance Report Template

## Monthly Turnover Performance Report Template

Purpose: give your team a consistent, data-driven way to review every turnover completed in the month: track performance against benchmarks, grade coordinators and vendors, surface trends, and give owners a clean, professional summary they can actually understand.
Cadence: generated by the 1st of each month covering the prior month's completed turnovers.
Audience: two versions, Internal Team Report and Owner-Facing Summary.

### Manager Notes

---
The internal report is for your team; the owner summary is for trust. Owners don't need to see coordinator scorecards or vendor ratings. They need to see that you're on top of it, their money was spent wisely, and what happens next.
Grading your own turnovers publicly builds massive owner confidence. Most property managers never show owners a performance grade. When you do, it signals accountability, and that's a powerful differentiator.
The vacancy cost tracker is your most powerful internal tool. When your team sees that a 4-day vendor delay cost an owner $148 in lost rent, turn-time becomes personal. Numbers change behavior.
Send the owner summary even when the turnover was perfect. A clean A-grade report with a fast turn-time and on-budget cost is a retention tool. It reminds owners exactly why they pay you.
Use the Red Flag Log as your SOP improvement engine. Every C or D grade is a process gap. If the same root cause appears twice, it's a system problem, not a people problem. Fix the system.
