# Pre-Move-Out Inspection Checklist

## Pre-Move-Out Inspection Checklist
Conducted 1-2 Weeks Before Move-Out Date — [Company Name]
Property: ____________________   Unit: ________   Inspector: ____________________   Date: __________
Purpose: give the tenant a fair opportunity to correct issues before the final inspection, reduce deposit disputes, and set clear expectations. This is a walkthrough WITH the tenant, not a final judgment.
Check state law: some states require offering a pre-move-out inspection (for example California). Consult legal counsel on local requirements for Tennessee and Georgia properties.
### Before the Inspection

---
☐ Send tenant written notice of pre-move-out inspection date, time, and purpose
☐ Confirm tenant's move-out date in writing
☐ Pull and review the original move-in inspection report and photos for comparison
☐ Prepare a copy of the tenant's move-out responsibilities checklist to bring to the walkthrough
☐ Confirm inspection software or app is ready
☐ Confirm inspector has access instructions (key, lockbox, tenant letting them in)

### After the Inspection

---
☐ Review all flagged issues with the tenant in person during the walkthrough
☐ Provide tenant with a written summary of all items needing correction before move-out
☐ Give tenant a clear deadline to address each item (typically the move-out date)
☐ Inform tenant which items, if unresolved, will result in security deposit deductions
☐ Upload all photos and inspection notes to the property management platform
☐ Share the pre-move-out report with the tenant via portal or email
☐ Note normal wear and tear vs. tenant-caused damage - do not charge for wear and tear
☐ Notify owner of any significant issues identified
### Manager Notes

---
The pre-move-out inspection is a dispute prevention tool, not a gotcha. Tenants who know exactly what is expected before they leave are far less likely to dispute deposit deductions. Document everything in writing and always compare back to the move-in report.
